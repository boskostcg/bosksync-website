BoskSync by BOSKOS respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website or software.

We may collect information such as your name, email address, eBay account details, order information, delivery addresses and tracking information when you use BoskSync.

Information is used to provide BoskSync services, manage orders, prepare shipping labels, upload tracking information, improve the software and communicate with users.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.
